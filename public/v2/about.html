About us

We only win when you're fully booked.

GetLeadFlow exists for one reason: to take client acquisition completely off a detailer's plate — so the people who are brilliant at coating cars can stop being part-time marketers.

Why we exist

Great detailers lose to worse detailers who market better.

It's the most frustrating thing in this industry. The shop with the best coatings sits quiet while the shop down the road — who cuts corners — stays booked, because they answer faster and run better ads.

We built GetLeadFlow to fix exactly that. Not to sell you "leads" and disappear, but to run the entire acquisition engine end to end: the ads that attract real buyers, the CRM that catches every lead, and a team that talks to them and books the job. You get the full calendar. You just coat the cars.
